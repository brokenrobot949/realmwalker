Realm Codex

The basics. You guide a single hero through an idle realm — progress continues while the game is closed.
Your class. At creation you choose one of three: the Squire (martial, sword & shield), the Scout (martial, bow & daggers), or the Apprentice (magical, staff & wand). Each grants a small permanent bonus, and decides which evolutions open to you later.
Class evolutions. At level 20 your class evolves into one of two paths, and at level 40 it evolves again into one of two further paths — eighteen evolved classes in all, six at the second tier and twelve at the third. Each evolution grants a stronger permanent bonus that replaces your previous class bonus rather than stacking, plus a new weapon and a new repertoire of skills. The choice is permanent for that hero.
Skills. At levels 10, 25, 30, 45, 50, and 55 you choose a new skill from your current class's repertoire — six in all. Each is triggered from the Skills tab for a temporary effect, or an instant one like mending an injury, then goes on cooldown; the stronger the skill, the longer the wait before it's ready again.
Questing Zones. Pick a zone under Adventure to quest passively for XP, gold, and item drops; a questing session caps at 8 hours, but you may stop questing at any time with no penalty — XP and drops are banked continuously as you go.
Dungeons, Raids, Campaign. Timed ventures on the Adventure tab that resolve after a set duration, win or lose; the Campaign advances chapter by chapter. Renown comes from these and the Arena — never from questing.
The Forgotten Beyond. A level-60 expedition past the seam in the world, where time does not keep step with the realm. Its length is not fixed and is not yours to choose: the span is rolled when you set out, anywhere from thirty minutes to six hours, and nothing shortens it — not haste, not a swiftness buff, not power far beyond what the place demands. XP, gold, and renown all scale with the span you drew, so a long crossing is a rich one. It yields no ordinary gear whatsoever. What it may yield is a Mythic weapon, a rarity above Legendary that exists nowhere else in the realm — not in any zone, dungeon, raid, chapter, or shop. The longer the rolled span, the better the odds, from 5% at the half-hour floor to a hard ceiling of 75% at six hours. An expedition that fails returns with nothing mythic, however long it was gone.
The Arena. Fights resolve every 10 seconds against escalating monsters, and there is no retreat — you fight until a wound or a beating carries you out. Wins bank XP, gold, and renown, and the longer you last, the bigger the survival bonus on your spoils (up to +50%).
World Bosses. A ladder of ten things the realm never managed to kill, opened only once your hero has seen the Campaign through to its last chapter. You climb it one rung at a time: only the next unbeaten name is ever shown to you, and the one after it stays nameless until the one before it falls. They yield no gear and no gold whatsoever — only XP and renown far beyond anything else in the realm. They are also brutally dangerous whether you win or lose: a 35% chance of injury on a kill, and 85% on a defeat. Do not expect to reach the top of the ladder. The point is how far you get, and the Hall remembers by name whoever first felled each one, across every life you live.
Injuries & Rest. Injuries lock you out of adventuring at 2; rest to heal — a Short Rest (4 hours) heals one injury, a Long Rest (6 hours) heals both.
Outgrowing an expedition. Once your power overwhelms a dungeon, raid, or chapter, the extra strength stops buying better odds — the danger is already as good as beaten — and starts buying speed and safety instead. A venture you have thoroughly outgrown is marched through faster and is far less likely to leave a mark on you, up to three-quarters less likely. The duration shown on each card already accounts for it. But an old road pays an old road's toll: as a place stops being able to threaten you, its spoils dwindle toward what such a place was ever worth. This turns on the danger, not on the date — an expedition that still tests you yields its full measure however far your name has travelled since, while one you can walk through in your sleep gives up only what a sleepwalk deserves. Plunder worth having is found ahead of you, never behind.
The odds of a wound. A dungeon, raid, or campaign chapter carries a 10% chance of injury on success, and a steep 60% chance on failure. The Arena is gentler at first but grows crueler with every win — its injury chance climbs 3% per fight survived, and never strikes during an early run's safe fights. All these odds bend to your hero's own fortune: gear, race, class, renown, and legacy boons can all shift the chance of harm, for better or worse.
Town. The Shop restocks over time, the Blacksmith upgrades gear, the Cleric Temple grants blessings that mend injuries or shorten a vigil in exchange for tithe, the Tavern sells buffs, and the Enchanter binds properties into weapons and relics.
Salvage & the Enchanter. Instead of selling gear to the Shop for coin, salvage it instead for dust — the Enchanter's own currency. The Enchanter works only on weapons and relics: Inscribe grants a bonus-less weapon its first property, Reroll gambles a fresh random property onto a weapon or relic that already has one (it can land on the very same property again — that's the wager), and Choose spends far more dust to set the exact property you want instead of leaving it to chance. A relic's property is always the stronger of the two; a weapon's is a lesser echo of it.
Rebirth. Complete the Campaign to unlock the Rite of Rebirth: your hero is enshrined in the Hall of Legends, and a new hero rises bearing a permanent boon of your choice and a small gold inheritance. Boons stack across every life.
Achievements & Titles. Lifetime deeds — Arena wins, dungeons cleared, gold earned, and more — unlock permanent titles, each with its own small bonus. Every bonus you've unlocked stays active always; which title displays beside your name (Character > Titles) is purely cosmetic. Unlike renown titles, which reset to nothing every rebirth, achievement progress is tracked account-wide and carries across every life you live.
Saving. Your journey saves automatically to this browser; use the gear menu to export or import a backup, or to reset.
